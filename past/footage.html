Found Footage

Information

A camcorder was purchased at a garage sale that included an SD card containing unusual footage of unique locations. On the card, numerous photos and videos between February 2011 and January 2012 are found.

Footage

The footage itself is mysterious. The filmographer's identity remains unknown, though they may have been the salesperson from the aformentioned flea market. The locations seen in the media has no correlation to each other, along with them not being identified as real locations.

Archival

The contents of the camcorder are currently in the process of being archived. You may view the photo gallery here, or watch the videos here.
